# How to post the carousel on @playtableverse

## What to post
Five images, in this order: `slides/slide-01.png` to `slide-05.png`. Use the caption in `caption.md` and the alt text from the same file.

## Getting the pictures onto your phone
The files are 1080 x 1350, so Instagram will not shrink or crop them. Pick one way:
1. Download the five PNGs from the merged pull request's `marketing/instagram/first-post/slides/` folder on GitHub (open each file, then the download button), or
2. Send the folder to yourself with AirDrop, Google Drive, or e-mail to yourself, and save the images to your photo library.
Do not send them through WhatsApp or Messenger: those apps recompress the pictures and make the text soft.

## Posting from the Instagram app
1. Tap **+**, then **Post**.
2. Tap the **multi-select** icon (two squares) and tap the five slides in order, 1 to 5. The numbers on the thumbnails show the order.
3. Tap **Next**. Do not add a filter; leave every slide as is. If Instagram offers to crop, keep the 4:5 shape.
4. On the next screen, paste the caption. Add the hashtags at the end of the caption (or paste them as the first comment right after posting: both work).
5. Tap **Advanced settings** (or the three-dot menu), then **Write alt text**, and paste the alt text for each of the five slides (image 1 text for slide 1, and so on).
6. Skip location and tags. Tap **Share**.
7. Open the post, tap the three dots, and choose **Pin to your profile**.

## Posting from a computer (optional)
Meta Business Suite (business.facebook.com) lets you upload the five images, paste the caption and alt text, and schedule the post. It works only if the Instagram account is a Professional (creator or business) account and linked to a Facebook Page.

## Profile set-up before the first post
- **Profile picture:** `public/brand/png/social-profile-1080.png` (the Your Seat mark). Instagram shows it as a circle; the mark sits inside the safe area.
- **Name:** Tableverse. **Username:** playtableverse.
- **Bio (suggestion):** `Card and board games with your friends, in your browser. Friends beta. 💚`
- **Link:** leave it empty, or add a link only when there is something a visitor can use. The posts say we are in friends beta, so a link is not needed yet.
- **Highlights/Stories (optional):** use `cover-square-1080.png` for a Story that says the first post is live.

## Right after posting
- Check slide 1 on the profile grid and slide 5 on a phone in dark and light mode. Text must be readable without zooming.
- Open the post from a second account (or ask a friend) and confirm all five slides and the alt text are there.
- Reply to early comments the same day. Do not promise access or dates.
- Share the post to your own Story and send it to your friends beta group.

## What not to include
- No link to the game, no sign-up promise, no "launching" or "available now" wording.
- No mention of voice chat or features that have not been released.
- Do not edit the logo or recolour the images.
